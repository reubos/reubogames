Turn corners off and a cell keeps only its five edge neighbours, which is a gentler game and a different one: numbers then run 1 to 5. Either way it starts afresh.

The neighbourhood

The Cairo pentagon has two right angles and three of 120°. Four right angles make a full turn, so four pentagons pinwheel about each right-angled corner — that is the block of four, and blocks sit on a plain square grid.

A cell has five edges and so five neighbours by edge. Each of its two right-angled corners is shared with three others, and one of those three is reached only at that point. Five and two: seven.
