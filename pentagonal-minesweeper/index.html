Pentagonal Minesweeper

Minesweeper on the pentagonal tilings — the ones whose every tile is a pentagon. A cell's neighbours are the cells it touches, by an edge or merely at a corner, and how many that comes to is a property of the tiling rather than something the game gets to choose. On the square grid it is always eight. Here it is seven, or on the floret eight — but a count is not a shape, and seven neighbours arranged five ways make five different games.

Click to uncover. Right-click to plant a flag. Clicking a number that already has its full quota of flags beside it uncovers everything else it touches.

With that on, the board opens with enough cells already showing that counting, subsets and the mine total settle every last square. Turn it off for the plain game, where mines fall where they fall and the end can come down to a coin.
