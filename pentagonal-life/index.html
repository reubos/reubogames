The neighbourhood

Six pentagons meet at a hub to make a flower. Five of any cell's neighbours are the rest of its own flower — all six share the hub corner. The other three lie across its three short outer edges. Five and three: eight.

Counted the other way round, a cell has five edges and so five neighbours by edge, plus three more that only touch it at the hub. The square grid splits its eight four and four; this one splits them five and three.
