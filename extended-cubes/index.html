Extended Cuboid Simulator

Layout notation

Core size first, then one +face per stack of extra layers, outwards from the core:

  • 3x5x7: a plain cuboid (W×H×D)
  • +U: one layer the size of the core's U face; +2U: two of them
  • +F(5x4): a custom-size layer; +F(5x4,3x2): stacked layers of different sizes

Layer sizes leave out the face's own axis (U/D: W×D, R/L: H×D, F/B: W×H) and must match the core's parity on both sides, e.g. on the F face of a 3x4x5: 3x4, 5x4, 5x2, 1x2.

Layer:

Click a sticker to turn that layer clockwise (ctrl-click = anticlockwise, shift-click = double). Drag with the right mouse button to rotate the view; click the compass to reset it.
