HOW TO PLAY
Goal
Arrange all tiles back into their goal positions. Each tile has a target cell shown by its label (A1, B2, etc.).
Moving tiles
Click and drag a tile to slide it into an adjacent empty cell. Tiles cannot pass through barriers or blocked cells.
Row & column slides
When a row or column is completely full and has wrapping enabled, the ◀ ▶ or ▲ ▼ arrow buttons appear — click them to shift the entire row or column, wrapping tiles around to the other side.
Bandaged tiles
Tiles that span multiple rows or columns are bandaged together and always move as one piece. When a group of bandaged rows or columns can all slide together, a cyan line appears along the arrow area connecting them.
Editor
Click and drag across cells to draw a tile shape — all cells in one stroke form one tile. Toggle ↔ on a row for horizontal wrap, ↕ on a column for vertical wrap. Click the edge between two cells to place a barrier.
